FHD Orbit — derived bitmap family used for the Exo 2 menu option.

Based on Exo2-Variable.ttf from:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/exo2
Copyright 2013 The Exo 2 Project Authors
https://github.com/googlefonts/Exo-2.0
Licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; see Exo2-OFL.txt.

Native AFT glyphs use source weight 500 for body text and 600 for bold roles,
without synthetic expansion. The derived family is named FHD Orbit.
All Cyrillic/Latin body letters and digits come from the Exo 2 source outlines.
Missing symbols, including direction arrows, are from DejaVu Sans Regular;
see DejaVu-Regular-LICENSE.txt for copyright notices and its license.

Unmodified source: SOURCE/Fonts/Exo2-Variable.ttf.
Rebuild: python3 SOURCE/build_orbit_font.py (Pillow + fonttools required).
The game loads the included AFT files directly; no Windows font installation.
